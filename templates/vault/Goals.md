# Goals

Targets are grouped under the identity they belong to, because the thing that
changes behaviour is who you take yourself to be, not what you are trying to
achieve. Write the heading as "I am someone who ...".

Every target carries three things:

- a **cadence** in backticks — `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly`, `quarterly`, or a
  count like `3x-weekly`
- **when** — the implementation intention. A time and a place. A target without
  one is a wish.
- **floor** — the version that still counts on a bad day. Two minutes. If you
  never hit the floor, the floor is set too high.

`last:` is stamped for you, from evidence: when a task tagged `#goal/move` is
checked off in a daily note, the target tagged `#goal/move` gets the date. Do
not edit it by hand. A tag matching two targets stamps neither, on purpose.
